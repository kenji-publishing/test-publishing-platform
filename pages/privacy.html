7. Security

7.1 Technical Measures

  • Encryption - All communications are encrypted with SSL/TLS
  • Passwords - Hashed using bcrypt
  • Magic Links - One-time tokens, expire in 15 minutes
  • Security Questions - Answers stored encrypted
  • Access Control - Principle of least privilege

7.2 Operational Measures

  • Regular security audits
  • Employee security training
  • Incident response plan
  • Data backups
Note:
To maintain your account security, please store backup codes in a safe place and set security question answers that are difficult for others to guess.

11. Children's Privacy

This Service is intended for users aged 13 and older. We do not knowingly collect personal information from children under 13.

Users under 18 should use the Service with parental consent. Adult content is only displayed to users 18 and older.

If we become aware that we have collected information from a child under 13, we will promptly delete it.

12. International Data Transfer

Your information may be transferred to and stored on servers outside your country. Specifically in:

  • Japan
  • United States (AWS, OpenAI)
  • European Economic Area (EEA)

Appropriate data protection measures are in place at transfer destinations. For transfers outside the EEA, we use appropriate safeguards such as Standard Contractual Clauses (SCC).
